# About this copy

This is a modified copy of **Quant Terminal**, a free, open source local trading terminal by **Lewis Jackson** (MIT licence, see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)). Official source: https://part-timequant.com

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original author or by TradingView, Inc. It is not reverse-engineered from TradingView.

## What was changed
- The top promotional banner is replaced by an **IMIT OVR** framework banner, with a **Playbook** button.
- New files: `ovr-banner.js` (the installer for the banner), `public/js/ovr.js`, `public/css/ovr.css`.
- The Playbook content comes from the OVRstanding Group material: the loop, reading order, setups, tier sizing, trade management, all 19 states, the five sequences, how control shifts, and operator discipline.

Everything else is the original app. Educational only, not financial advice.
